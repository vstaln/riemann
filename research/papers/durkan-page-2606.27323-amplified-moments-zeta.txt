At present, asymptotic formulae of the shape (1.1) are known only for k = 1,2, the second
momentgoingbacktoHardyandLittlewood[16]andthefourthmomentgoingbacktoIngham
[22]. For larger k, sharp lower and upper bounds are known (the latter conditional on the
Riemann Hypothesis, due to Harper’s [17] refinement of Soundararajan’s method [32]), but
an asymptotic formula of the form (1.1) remains far beyond current methods. General lower-
bound methods originate in work of Ramachandra [29] and were sharpened by Rudnick and
Soundararajan [30], Radziwiłł and Soundararajan [28] and Heap and Soundararajan [19]. We
note that for the derivative of ζ(s) a sharp lower bound was obtained by Gao [14] for all real
k ≥ 0.
In fact, if the bound (1.1) were available for all positive integer k, this would immediately
imply the Lindelöf Hypothesis. Whilst bounds are available for all real k ≥ 0, in this paper we
shall be concerned solely with integer k. It is a well-studied problem to obtain good numerical
bounds for these moments, and an active area of research. This work improves upon a number
of these bounds, as asked in e.g. [15, Problem 6]. For a fuller account of the history of the
bounds obtained for moments of zeta, the reader is encouraged to consult Florea’s article [13].
