Title: Asymptotic absence of poles of Ihara zeta function of large Erdos-Renyi random graphs
Authors: O. Khorunzhiy
Published: 2021-01-09
Primary category: math.PR

ABSTRACT (fetched from arXiv export API):
Using recent results on the concentration of the largest eigenvalue and maximal vertex degree of large random graphs, we show that the infinite sequence of Erd\H os-Rényi random graphs $G(n,ρ_n/n)$ such that $ρ_n/\log n$ infinitely increases as $n\to\infty$ verifies a version of the graph theory Riemann Hypothesis.
