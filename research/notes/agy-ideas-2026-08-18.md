# Antigravity idea-generation batch — adjudication (2026-08-18)

## Source and status

One fresh Antigravity (`agy`) one-shot using Gemini 3.7 Flash High generated five direct-RH
candidate ideas. All source proposals were labeled CONJECTURED/INCONCLUSIVE by the generator.
This note records coordinator checks; no RH proof is claimed.

## Candidate adjudication

1. **Asymmetric theta-Mellin half-transform dominance** — **ABANDONED as stated**.
   The proposed orientation `|F_+(z)|>|F_+(-z)|` fails immediately at `z=iy` because the
   repo-proven `Phi(u)>0` gives `F_+(-iy)>F_+(iy)` for y>0. The reversed orientation was
   tested with Rust Simpson quadrature over 2,020 points (`x=0..50`, `y=.05..1`): it also
   fails on the grid at `(x,y)=(17,1)`, where
   `log(|F_+(-z)|/|F_+(z)|)=-0.004378834094`. This is a falsification probe, not an RH
   disproof. Tool: `tools/asym-theta-probe/`.

2. **Carleson-box energy for |zeta'/zeta|²** — **ABANDONED as stated**.
   The proposed universal threshold `mu(Q_R)<=pi R` fails on a local RH-compatible pole model:
   one on-line zero gives `0.551 pi R`, two separated on-line zeros give `1.483 pi R`, two
   close on-line zeros `2.174 pi R`, and a double on-line zero `2.205 pi R`. Tool:
   `tools/carleson-local-probe/`. This does not test zeta; it shows the proposed constant is
   too strong for the intended RH class.

3. **Prime-shift dissipative semigroup** — **INCONCLUSIVE/structurally deficient as stated**.
   The raw prime generator has coefficient mass `sum_p log(p)/p^sigma`, divergent for sigma<1,
   so its unweighted contractivity claim requires an unstated adapted domain/metric. The prior
   Rust prime-phase probe already refutes the simpler accretivity premise.

4. **Euler-Maclaurin residual angular Wronskian** — **CONJECTURED / INCONCLUSIVE**.
   It has a concrete residue-cancellation target and could be checked against DH, but the
   proposed implication from a conditional Wronskian sign to zero exclusion is not derived.
   No finite scan would establish its universal hypothesis; no implementation funded yet.

5. **Prime-scaling commutator Hamiltonian** — **ABANDONED as stated**.
   The proposed prime-compactified boundary conditions and spectral threshold are artificial,
   and no theorem connects its ground state to Xi zeros. Distributional commutators make the
   claimed gap domain-dependent.

## Net result

The Agy batch generated useful alternatives but no surviving one-way RH lemma. Rank 4 is the
only item not immediately falsified or structurally undefined, but it is currently a conjectural
implication without a certified control. The closure DAG and barrier-zoo firewall remain active.
