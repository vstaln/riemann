  A_pure(t0) = A(planted) − A(true)

  mean over Re≥1/2 arc minus mean over Re<1/2 arc of log|1−s/p|

  θ* = arccos((1/2 − c_re)/r) (arc endpoints where Re = 1/2); w = r/(p−c), u = e^{iθ*}/w,
  v = e^{−iθ*}/w. The constant Re log((p−c)/p) [+ log|w| for inside roots] is common to
  both arcs and cancels in the difference. Branch-free on the real part; Li₂ arguments
  always have modulus < 1 (principal branch, no cut issues).
