## 4. The analytic question — why WOULD curvature subtraction separate, and what theorem makes it unconditional

The intended (but, at code level, never built) mechanism. Let the zeros be the multiset
{ρ}. The Jensen functional E(c,r)=Σ_{|ρ−c|<r} log(r/|ρ−c|) is (minus) the logarithmic
potential of the zero measure. Along the critical line the zeros are self-reciprocal under
x↦1−x (ρ=1/2+iγ has reflected partner 1−(1/2+iγ)=1/2−iγ, i.e. the conjugate across Im=0).
For a center c=1/2+it on the line, the potential as a function of the real coordinate x is
therefore locally even about x=1/2, so its **first and odd-order derivatives, and in
particular the second-order (curvature) term ∂²E/∂x² evaluated at x=1/2, vanish identically
for on-line zeros**. Now place a single off-line zero at β+iγ (β>1/2). The functional
equation forces its reflected partner at (1−β)+iγ; an off-line *pair* {β,1−β} is still
symmetric about x=1/2, so an even-order moment cannot see it **only if** the center is at
x=1/2. The asymmetry only appears through a center *off* the line (as in g3-0, c_re=0.75):
then the two members {β,1−β} have *unequal* distances to the off-line center, and the
log-potential gradient/curvature picks up a term ≈ (β−1/2)·(c_re−1/2)/r² — a smoking-gun
sign/magnitude that is strictly zero for genuinely on-line zeros regardless of center. A
**Turan/de-Branges-type strict-positivity theorem** asserting that this second-order
(curvature) Jensen–Turan functional has a fixed sign for every β≠1/2, for all t, and with a
magnitude ≥ some uniform bound ρ(|β−1/2|)>0, would make the separator unconditional.

Why the claim fails here, honestly: (i) **no such code exists** — the shipping binary
computes only the raw first-order E and reports the single-plant toy log(r/d0) with E_RH=0
*by construction*; no curvature term, no null distribution, no threshold are ever evaluated.
(ii) Even the *mathematics* is not a separator as implemented: a disc centered on the
critical line at x=1/2 cannot see an off-line pair by any even moment, and a disc centered
off the line gives E_RH=0 vacuously — that is precisely the configuration (c_re=0.75,r=0.2)
producing the headline gap. (iii) Proving the strict-sign theorem for all β≠1/2
is circular-equivalent to establishing the desired zero distribution, so a *numeric* gap at
discrete points, one radius, and one height is not evidence for it. When the disc is made
non-vacuous (2c), the gap is not positive but **negative** (−0.531), i.e. the screening
against off-line implants is refuted at the tested non-vacuous parameters.
