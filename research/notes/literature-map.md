### 1c. The chain to 2/3 and 67.25% — synthesis

C states explicitly (Remark 1.1 and §1.2) that the 2/3 and the 0.6725 are **Montgomery's / Montgomery–Taylor's RH-conditional constants made unconditional**:
- 2/3 = H(1) = 2 − 1/λ − λ/3 at λ = 1 (C (1.3)); "The constant 2/3 (resp. 0.6725) is Montgomery's (resp. Montgomery–Taylor's) constant for simple zeros under the Riemann hypothesis, and the arithmetic input is exactly Montgomery's prime-side evaluation of the pair-correlation second moment with band-width ≤ 1, which is unconditional" (C abstract).
- 0.6725 = 2 − 1/c*₁ = 3/2 − (1/√2)cot(1/√2) (C Theorem D), with 1/c*₁ = 1/2 + 2^{−1/2}cot(2^{−1/2}) = 1.3274992… "the Montgomery–Taylor constant [Mon75, CG93]" (C §7.1).
- B25 shows the same 67.25% appears **under the box hypothesis** at box width b = 0.001 (Theorem 2: N^s(B_{0.001}) ≥ (0.67250064+o(1))·T/2π·log T) — i.e. the Montgomery–Taylor constant is what the pair-correlation method delivers when the off-line excursions are squeezed to zero width. C then removes even that hypothesis.

Missing links in our sources: the exact constants for BCY 2011 (41.05%?) and Feng 2012 (41.28%?) are NOT stated in C/B24/B25 (Feng's number comes only from the round brief). Also Selberg's unpublished work and Montgomery–Taylor's provenance ([Mon74] vs [Mon75]) are cited with inconsistent IDs across B24 and B25/C.

## 2. Ingredient-by-ingredient trace (the 67.25% proof)

The proof chain in N is: define φ_T, ν_T = µ + λ_T + π_T, v_ρ, W_T; then
`#on ≥ rank W_on ≥ 2trW_on + 4trW_off − 4n₊(W_off) − ||W_T||²_HS  (N Lemma 3.4)`
`= 4trW_T − 2(trW_on + 2n₊(W_off)) − ||W_T||²_HS ≥ 4trW_T − 2N_all − ||W_T||²_HS  (N Claim 2.3)`
`= (4 − 2 − 1/2 − (1/√2)cot(1/√2))·N_all  (N Lemmas 3.2 & 3.3) = (3/2 − (1/√2)cot(1/√2))·N_all`.

## 3. What the BGST papers actually prove

### B24 (arXiv:2306.04799, "An unconditional Montgomery theorem…", 13 pp.)

**Main theorem (B24 Theorem 1)** — the unconditional Montgomery form factor. With zeros counted with multiplicity,
F(α) := (T/2π·logT)⁻¹ Σ_{ρ,ρ′: 0<γ,γ′≤T} T^{α(ρ−ρ′)}·w(ρ−ρ′), w(u) := 4/(4−u²):
"F(α) is real, even, and nonnegative. Moreover, as T → ∞, F(α) = T^{−2α}(log T + O(1)) + α + O(1/√logT) uniformly for 0 ≤ α ≤ 1."
The key: the zero sum runs over **all complex zeros** ρ = β + iγ (δ = β − 1/2 retained), whereas Montgomery's original assumed RH to drop the β's. "Theorem 1 is nearly identical to Montgomery's theorem in [Mon73] and [GM87, Lemma 8] except it does not assume RH".

**Theorem 2** (simple zeros under a thin-box hypothesis): if all zeros with T^{3/8} < γ ≤ T satisfy |β − 1/2| < 1/(2 log T), then ≥ 61.7% of the zeros (with T^{3/8} < γ ≤ T) are simple. "The method of proof neither requires nor provides any information on whether any of these zeros are or are not on the critical line."

**Theorem 3** (same 61.7% under a strong zero-density hypothesis): N(σ,T) = o(T^{2(1−σ)}) for 1/2 + 1/(2 log T) ≤ σ ≤ 25/32 + η, η > 0 fixed — citing Bourgain's [Bou00] estimate N(σ,T) = o(T^{2(1−σ)}) for σ ≥ 25/32 + η.

**Method (one paragraph).** Rewrite the pair-correlation sum as an L²-norm (Lemma 3): F(x,T) = (2/π)∫|Σ_ρ x^{ρ−1/2}/(1−(ρ−(1/2+it))²)|²dt, using the residue integral ∫dt/((1+t²)(1+(t+a)²)) = 2π/(4+a²). Then evaluate the zero sum via the explicit formula (Landau [Lan09]) with the σ ↔ 1−σ subtraction trick (B24 Lemma 1), keeping the real parts δ and bounding the error by the zero-free region (Korobov–Vinogradov η(t) = c/(log t)^{2/3}(log log t)^{1/3}). The prime-side integral R(x,T) is evaluated unconditionally (Montgomery's [Mon73] computation in the form of [GM87, Lemma 8]): R(x,T) = x^{−2}T·logT(logT + O(1)) + T(log x + O(√logT)). Then a Tsang kernel K(z) with K̂(t) = j(2πt)·sech(2πt), j = Fejér or Montgomery–Taylor, whose key properties (B24 Lemma 6, from [Tsa93, Lemma 1]) are K(x) > 0 on ℝ and Re K(x+iy) > 0 for |y| < 1, converts the F-evaluation into a positive sum over close pairs |β−β′| < 1/log T (B24 Lemma 7), from which Σ m_ρ ≤ (4/3+o(1))·N via the multiplicity/diagonal argument (B24 §7), giving 2 − (constant) ≥ 0.6086 (Fejér) or 0.6175 (Montgomery–Taylor) proportion simple.

### B25 (arXiv:2501.14545v2, "Pair correlation of zeros of the Riemann zeta function I: proportions of simple zeros and critical zeros", 16 pp.)

**Main theorems.** Box B_b := {s: 1/2 − b/(2 log T) < σ < 1/2 + b/(2 log T), T < t ≤ 2T}; assume all zeros with T < γ ≤ 2T lie in B_b.

**Method (one paragraph).** Generalise Montgomery's F to sums over zeros in (T, 2T] with complex weights x^{ρ−ρ′}W(ρ−ρ′) (W(u) = 4/(4−u²)): the corrected "Montgomery Theorem (MT)": F(x,T) = (T/2πx²)·log²T·(1+O(1/√logT)) + (T/2π)·log x + O(T√logT) for 1 ≤ x ≤ T (B25 (2.3), proven in §3 by splitting F = F_{(0,2T]} − F_{(0,T]} and bounding the cross term via the Korobov–Vinogradov zero-free region). Then a **Tsang kernel with a width parameter b**: K_b(z) with K̂_b(t) = j(2πt)/cosh(2πbt) (B25 (4.5), (4.6)); Lemma 3 (generalising [Tsa93, Lemma 1]) gives Re K_b(x+iy) > 0 for |y| < b and K_b(z) ≪ e^{|Im z|}/(1+|z|²), proven via the Fourier pair cosh(2πyt)/cosh(2πbt) (B25 Lemma 2). Lemma 4 evaluates the positive sum: Σ_{ρ,ρ′∈B_b} Re K_b(−i(ρ−ρ′)logT) = (1/2π)(j(0) + 2∫₀¹ αj(α)/cosh(bα)dα + O(1/√logT))·T/2π·logT. The box assumption makes |β−β′| < b/log T, so every term is positive and the weight W can be removed (error ≪ e^b·T). The diagonal (ρ = ρ′) and **symmetric diagonal (ρ′ = 1 − ρ, off the line)** terms then give the lower bound (6.1); with j_F and b → 0 this yields the fundamental inequality
  **Σ m_ρ + Σ_{β≠1/2} m_ρ ≤ (4/3 + o(1))·T/2π·logT** (B25 (6.2))
  — the diagonal sum is ≥ Σ m_ρ ≥ N and the symmetric-diagonal sum is exactly the off-line multiplicity sum, so the off-line total ≤ (1/3+o(1))N. For fixed b, cosh(bα) is retained and C_b(j) := [j(0) + 2∫₀¹αj(α)/cosh(bα)dα]/[2∫₀¹j(α)/cosh(bα)dα] replaces 4/3 (B25 (7.1), (7.2)): N^s, N⁰ ≥ (2 − C_b(j))·T/2π·logT, N^s₀ ≥ (3 − 2C_b(j))·T/2π·logT; j_M is chosen to minimise C_b (Table 1).
