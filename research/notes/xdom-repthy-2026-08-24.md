## 1. Mechanism — the representation-theoretic decomposition of the CUE content

The CUE content of the layer-(b) claim decomposes into THREE tiers. Only tier 1 is
representation theory; tiers 2–3 are where the arithmetic layer lives.

Tier 1 — the model constants m_b are symmetric-group / Schur-Weyl objects (PROVEN, completed).
For a Haar-random N×N unitary U, the spectral moments are averages of products of traces.
By Schur-Weyl duality, traces of powers of U decompose over the symmetric group: the algebra
generated by U enters through the central idempotents indexed by partitions of b, and the
averages evaluate to sums over permutations with weights given by the **Weingarten function**
(g_r, a rational function of N obtained by inverting the Gram matrix of the symmetric group
action). Hence:
    m_b(N) = E[ (1/N) tr(U^b) ... ] = Σ_{π ∈ S_b} (integer incl. sign) · (Weingarten coeff)
is an EXACT RATIONAL in N. This is precisely why m7(8)=2427905/131072 is rational, why every
order-N fit closes with DELTA=0, and why the finite-N log law (Morris integral, §Refinements-1
of the referee note) is exactly computable. **Representation theory is the *only* theory that
explains the rationals; there is nothing zeta-specific here, and nothing more to extract on
this tier.** This tier is done and fully consistent with our confirmation.

The same S_b/Weingarten count reappears as the CUE n-level density: the k-point correlation
of the sine/circular model is a single determinant (determinantal, by the sine-kernel
Fredholm determinant), and zonal polynomials of the appropriate partition govern the
moments-of-moments. So the *right-hand* tower (1, 4/3, 2, 346/105, ...) is one coherent
SCHUR-WEYL object. It is the "diagonal" in every sense.

Tier 2 — the arithmetic moment splits diagonal + off-diagonal (PROVEN; the diagonal is
CUE-identical for ALL b; the off-diagonal is the entire open content).
Write the normalized spectral measure of the zeta operator / the pair-correlation tower as a
sum over the divisors/character terms of the explicit formula. The b-th trace moment is
    m_b(T) = [CUE part] + [off-diagonal arithmetic part].
The CUE part is the Weingarten/S_b count of Tier 1, and Rudnick–Sarnak's combinatorial
matching (PROVEN, literature) shows the two coincide on the diagonal EXACTLY — the b-th
correlation's predicted density IS the CUE density by construction, term-for-term on the
diagonal. The off-diagonal part consists of prime/character/divisor sums (the "arithmetic
factor" a_b · (Euler product), in the Farmer–Gonek–Hughes/Keating–Snaith language). For b=2,
in the band-limited form Montgomery uses, the off-diagonal vanishes unconditionally — this is
the ONLY tier-2 case closed, and it is exactly why the referee note says "Montgomery enters
ONLY here." For b>=3 the off-diagonal is the **n-level correlation conjecture
(Rudnick–Sarnak / Conrey–Farmer–Zirnbauer)**: OPEN, unconditional in essentially no
band beyond b=2, and — decisively — it is a sum of *prime and character terms*, not a
unitary integral. Schur-Weyl has nothing to say about Σ Λ(n)^b n^{-σ} or the character sums;
they live outside the group-algebra of S_b. (PROVEN structural statement, not a value claim.)

Tier 3 — the equivalence/reframing that representation theory DOES give us (CONJECTURED).
The open tower admits a Toeplitz/determinantal hologram: the CUE side is a determinantal
point process (sine kernel), and by Borodin–Okounkov the corresponding n-level/Toeplitz
determinants are Fredholm determinants of the sine kernel. In the repo's own language this is
a TOEPLITZ total-positivity statement (ASW/Edrei, cf. `total-positivity-2026-08-15.md`).
Therefore the layer-(b) claim for ALL b simultaneously is equivalent to:
    [arithmetic n-level correlation kernel is asymptotically the sine kernel / the
     arithmetic spectral measure is asymptotically determinantal with sine asymptotics]
The interest of this Tier-3 statement is that it DECIDES the layer all-orders-in-one-stroke
(one determinantal/Toeplitz theorem does b=2,3,4,... together), vs. the paper's b-by-b rung
statement. That is a genuine *reduction* that a static reader can verify moves beyond
"restating". BUT it is an equivalence to the same open object, correctly labeled CONJECTURED:
proving asymptotic determinantal structure for the zeta side is the n-correlation conjecture,
not a representation-theoretic input. Tier 3 REHOSTS the weak link; it does not remove it.

## 2. Why it could decide m_b(T)->m_b (the weak link) rather than restate it

Two decision paths, honestly graded.

Path A (all-orders determinantal / Toeplitz-TP gate) — CONJECTURED, no proof.
If one could establish the Tier-3 asymptotic determinantal structure (equivalently the
relevant Toeplitz minors keep the CUE-type alternating/positive signature at ALL orders),
then `m_b(T)->m_b` holds on a single theorem, not a b-by-b ladder. This is the only framing
under which representation theory is doing more than restating: it converts an infinite
family of moment claims into ONE Toeplitz-determinant-sign claim, testable at finite order
exactly the way the repo already test Toeplitz TP. Nothing we read proves this; the 
determinantal assumption is the conjecture. So Path A is a *reduction lever*, valuable as a
completeness reformulation, NOT a theorem.

Path B (asymptotic freeness) — CONJECTURED / largely a red herring, discussed for honesty.
One might hope the arithmetic spectral measure is "asymptotically free in the same sense" as
the sine/CUE spectrum, making all mixed moments collapse by a free central-limit-style lemma.
This is where the domain lens is most seductive and least supported: the zeta zero process is
CONJECTURED to converge to the *sine process*, which is determinantal and NOT a free
(operator-algebra) limit in the Voiculescu sense — it is a *determinantal point process*, a
different structure. There is no evidence the zeta spectral measure is canonically free, and
the CARLEMAN/determinacy route already covers growth via (b!)^2 (referee §53: uses only the
UPPER bound, direction-safe). Path B adds no decision power beyond Path A. Honest label:
INCONCLUSIVE/discarded as non-decisive; do not fund it.

What both paths share, and why it is a real (if negative) advance: they make PRECISE the
claim that *nothing on the CUE side can decide the weak link*, because the CUE side is a
closed Schur-Weyl sector (Tier 1) that is provably disjoint from the arithmetic sector
(Tier 2 off-diagonal). A future attacker/reviewer who wants to break the capstone must attack
the arithmetic off-diagonal (NT), and holding a representation-theoretic sharpness certificate
lets us state that *confidently* instead of waving at it. The layer-(b) claim is NOT a
statement any RMT/Schur identity can certify; it is exactly a number-theoretic conjecture, and
the capstone's soundness-as-written depends on running it as one.
