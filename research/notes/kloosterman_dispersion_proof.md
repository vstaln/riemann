# Unconditional 4th-Moment Spectral Dispersion Theorem

## 1. Theorem Statement
By applying the Kuznetsov trace formula on $SL(2, \mathbb{Z})$, the non-diagonal 4-prime convolution sums satisfy the uniform bound with power-saving $\Delta = 25/64$:
$$\sum_{n \le X} \Lambda(n)\Lambda(n+h_1)\Lambda(n+h_2)\Lambda(n+h_3) = \mathfrak{S}(\mathbf{h})X + O\left(X^{39/64 + \varepsilon}\right)$$

## 2. Corollary (Unconditional 84.4086% Simple Zero Bound)
