## 4. What a real Beurling-hybrid disc-average separation would mean — the missing theorem

Context that would make a local disc statistic relevant to RH at all: the
Nyman–Baez–Duarte criterion, RH ⟺ d_N → 0, where d_N² = dist²(1, span{θ_k/x}) in L²(0,1),
θ_k = {1/kx} (Beurling: RH ⟺ span{{θ/x}: θ>1} dense). The planted-defect floor
(`beurling_defect_floor.rs`) supplies the only genuine RH-relevant structure in this family:
an off-line zero β+iγ forces d_N² ≥ C(β,γ)/N^{2(1−β)} with C from the |ζ′| residue — a
real, checkable prediction (that binary computes exact Gram matrices for N≤200 and fits
slopes; it is the honest sibling and was not part of this audit's verdict).

A **disc-average Jensen separation** would mean RH if one proved:

> **THEOREM (missing; call it the Jensen–Beurling local-defect lower bound).**
> Let N be a zero multiset closed under the functional equation ρ ↦ 1−ρ̄ (off-line zeros
> occur in pairs {β+iγ, 1−β+iγ}, β>1/2), D a disc centered on the critical line
> c = 1/2+it₀ with r < 1/2, and J_N(D) = Σ_{ρ∈N∩D} log(r/|ρ−c|). Then there exists a
> universal κ>0 such that for every off-line pair at height γ₀,
> J_N(D) − J_RH(D) ≥ κ·(β−1/2)²/r² · 1_{|γ₀−t₀|<r},
> where J_RH is the value under RH, and the bound holds **uniformly in t₀** over an
> infinite family of discs.

The structure that could make this true is the self-reciprocity of off-line pairs: a center
on Re=1/2 sees {β, 1−β} symmetrically in even moments, so the leading β−1/2 asymmetry must
enter through odd moments or a pair-distance imbalance — that is the only mechanism that
would give a sign-definite, RH-sensitive term. The missing ingredients, precisely:
(i) a **two-sided log-potential bound** for off-line pairs in terms of β−1/2 (nothing beyond
the trivial single-plant log(r/d0) exists today), and
(ii) **uniformity in t₀**: a finite scan over T cannot certify "for all zeros" — one needs
the inequality for every disc of an infinite family, which in turn requires regularizing the
log-potential (subtract a background zero-density term; the bare sum is not absolutely
convergent), plus a
(iii) **local-global bridge**: a quantitative duality d_N² ≥ f(E(c,r)) linking the local
Jensen mass to the global Báez-Duarte distance (or its Jensen-weighted Gram version). Without
(iii), no finite-N finite-T numeric gap can be promoted to a statement about RH.

Neither (i), (ii), nor (iii) exists in code or in the wave-90 notes. The binary ships the toy
single-plant log(r/d0), a hardcoded "calibration", and an arbitrary k=0.04 "bound".
