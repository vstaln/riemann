adaptively with a world's own zero positions separate the RH world (true ζ) from
the RH-false controls (DH mod-5, planted-zero Beurling, Epstein cls-2)?

packets tuned to the world's own zero imaginary parts**, h_j(x) = window_B(x)·cos(γ_j x).

**Baseline reproduced first:** the fixed-grid wave-25 probe runs unchanged and
gives the ledger's non-separation (e.g. B=2.5,M=8: RH −0.8691 vs DH −0.8532;
