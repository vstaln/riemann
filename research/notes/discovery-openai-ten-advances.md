## The transferable result: sphere packing (#1)

The Cohn–Elkies LP bound is the **structural analog of our certificate class**: an admissible test
function (radial, real, Fourier-nonneg) whose LP-optimized quotient gives an upper bound. The Lean
formalization (SpherePacking.lean) contains the exact evaluation:

- `linear_program_root`: fullLinearProgram(d)^{1/d} → √(e/2π), i.e. the LP optimum's exact asymptotic
  rate — base-2 exponent ½·log₂(2π/e) ≈ **0.604400544…** — *better* than the classical Kabatiansky–
  Levenshtein 0.599, "reaching the Cohn–Elkies threshold" by finding the LP's true optimum.
- `root_before_infimum`: sInf of the admissible quotient^{1/d}/√d → 1/π.
- Method: **Mellin-transform evaluation of the radial Fourier LP** (`mellinFrequency_eq_fourier`, gamma
  products, integer/half-integer gamma product identities).
