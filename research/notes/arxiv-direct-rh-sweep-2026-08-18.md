## Relevant results

### 2608.11520 — Contour Hankel dynamics and indicator fields for the Riemann Xi-function

Status: **CLOSED (PROVEN equivalence)**. The paper's Theorem 3.4 gives exact real-symmetric
Hankel inertia: a nonreal conjugate pair contributes one negative direction once matrix order
is high enough. Corollary 3.7 explicitly states PSD over every real-centered disk is equivalent
to RH; Remark 3.8 says independent positivity remains unresolved. Already recorded separately in
`arxiv-2608.11520-hankel-triage-2026-08-18.md`.

### 2607.04316 — A Gaussian–Perron Prime-Side Defect and Local Profiles Near Critical-Line Zeros

Status: **NOT A DIRECT-RH SUFFICIENT CONDITION**. The abstract says the local profile theorem is
proved under the Riemann Hypothesis and pole-damping/contour-regularity hypotheses. It is a
local diagnostic, not an unconditional zero-exclusion lemma. No probe funded.

### 2607.04338 — A proof of Riemann's hypothesis via Hadamard–Weierstrass factorization

Status: **WITHDRAWN**. The current arXiv abstract page explicitly states: “Mistakes in main
approach. Withdrawn.” The PDF/source is unavailable for the withdrawn version. It is not a

### 2607.16795 — An explicit uniform cubic wedge for consecutive Toeplitz minors of the Riemann xi coefficients

Status: **NOT A DIRECT-RH LEVER**. The abstract itself says the result concerns only the tail
`k >> r^3` and “makes no progress on the Riemann Hypothesis,” whose difficulty lies in the
complementary region. This is a tail asymptotic consistent with the already closed PF/GJT
completion family.

### 2607.25002 — How Random Is the Möbius Function? Smoothing, Probability, and the RH

Status: **EQUIVALENCE/EXPOSITORY**. The abstract explicitly presents smoothed forms of classical
Mertens/Mobius criteria equivalent to RH, plus explanatory probabilistic/dynamical material.
It does not supply a new one-way condition with an RH-false control.

## Decision
