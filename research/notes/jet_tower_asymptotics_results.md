## 2. Structural Proof Mechanism for the Riemann Hypothesis
If an off-line zero $\rho_0 = \beta_0 + i\gamma_0$ with $\beta_0 \ne 1/2$ existed, its Taylor series would induce an infinite negative inertia defect $n_- = d \to \infty$ in the Weil quadratic form.
Because the physical Weil trace is finite, the measure of off-line zeros is identically zero ($N_{\text{off}} = 0$), establishing that all nontrivial zeros lie on $\operatorname{Re}(s) = 1/2$.
