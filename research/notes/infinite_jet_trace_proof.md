## 2. Formulation of the Infinite Jet Bundle $\mathcal{J}_\infty$ & Operator $W_\infty$

In the Fourier / time domain, the evaluation atom for the $a$-th derivative of $\xi(1/2 + i\gamma)$ corresponds to:
$$v^{(a)}(t) = t^a v(t) e^{i\gamma t} = t^a \cos(\sqrt{2}t) e^{i\gamma t}.$$

### 2.1 The Weighted Bargmann-Fock Jet Space
We define the infinite jet space $\mathcal{J}_\infty = \bigoplus_{k=0}^\infty \mathcal{H}_k$ equipped with analytic convergence weights $w_k$:
$$w_k = \frac{(2\pi)^{2k}}{(2k)!}, \quad k \ge 0.$$
These weights satisfy the generating identity:
$$\sum_{k=0}^\infty w_k t^{2k} = \sum_{k=0}^\infty \frac{(2\pi t)^{2k}}{(2k)!} = \cosh(2\pi t).$$
On the domain $t \in [-1/2, 1/2]$, $\cosh(2\pi t)$ is smooth, positive, and uniformly bounded by $\cosh(\pi) \approx 11.591953275$.

## 3. Proof 1: Nuclear Trace-Class Property & Finite Physical Trace

### 3.1 Statement & Analytical Formulation
**Theorem 1.** The operator $W_\infty$ generated by $\mathcal{K}_\infty(t, s)$ on $L^2([-1/2, 1/2])$ is a self-adjoint, positive semidefinite, nuclear trace-class operator ($\mathcal{S}_1$) with finite trace given by the Sobolev $H^1$ norm of $v$:
$$\operatorname{Tr}(W_\infty) = \int_{-1/2}^{1/2} \mathcal{K}_\infty(t, t) \, dt = \int_{-1/2}^{1/2} \cosh(2\pi t^2) \cos^2(\sqrt{2}t) \, dt < \infty.$$

### 3.2 Proof
1. **Symmetry & Positivity:**  
   For any test function $f \in L^2([-1/2, 1/2])$:
   $$\langle f, W_\infty f \rangle = \int_{-1/2}^{1/2} \int_{-1/2}^{1/2} \mathcal{K}_\infty(t, s) f(t) \overline{f(s)} \, dt \, ds = \sum_{k=0}^\infty w_k \left| \int_{-1/2}^{1/2} t^k v(t) f(t) \, dt \right|^2 \ge 0.$$
   Thus $W_\infty \succeq 0$ is strictly positive semidefinite.
2. **Mercer Eigenfunction Decomposition:**  
   Since $\mathcal{K}_\infty(t, s) \in C^\infty([-1/2, 1/2] \times [-1/2, 1/2])$ is a continuous Mercer kernel, Mercer's Theorem guarantees the existence of an orthonormal basis $\{\phi_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ with eigenvalues $\lambda_n \ge 0$ such that:
   $$\mathcal{K}_\infty(t, s) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty \lambda_n \phi_n(t) \phi_n(s), \quad \text{converging absolutely and uniformly}.$$
3. **Trace Class (Nuclearity):**  
   The trace norm (nuclear norm) equals the sum of eigenvalues:
   $$\|W_\infty\|_{\mathcal{S}_1} = \operatorname{Tr}(W_\infty) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty \lambda_n = \int_{-1/2}^{1/2} \mathcal{K}_\infty(t, t) \, dt.$$
   Substituting $\mathcal{K}_\infty(t, t) = \cosh(2\pi t^2) \cos^2(\sqrt{2}t)$:
   $$\operatorname{Tr}(W_\infty) \le \cosh\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) \int_{-1/2}^{1/2} \cos^2(\sqrt{2}t) \, dt = \cosh\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) \|v\|_{L^2}^2 < \infty.$$

## 4. Proof 2: Off-Line Pairs Induce Infinite Sylvester Signature $(d, d, 0)$

### 4.1 Pairing under Functional Equation
Let $\rho_0 = 1/2 + \delta_0 + i\gamma_0$ ($\delta_0 > 0$) be a hypothetical off-line zero of $\xi(s)$. By the functional equation $\xi(s) = \xi(1-s)$ and Schwarz reflection $\xi(\bar{s}) = \overline{\xi(s)}$, $\rho_0$ is paired with:
$$1 - \bar{\rho}_0 = 1/2 - \delta_0 + i\gamma_0.$$

Differentiating the functional equation $\xi(1-s) = \xi(s)$ repeatedly:
$$\frac{d^a}{ds^a} \xi(1-s) = (-1)^a \xi^{(a)}(s).$$

### 4.2 Decoupling on the Jet Truncation $\mathcal{V}_d$
Define the $2d$-dimensional evaluation subspace:
$$\mathcal{V}_d = \operatorname{span}\left\{ u_+^{(a)}(t) = t^a e^{\delta_0 t} v(t) e^{i\gamma_0 t}, \quad u_-^{(a)}(t) = t^a e^{-\delta_0 t} v(t) e^{i\gamma_0 t} : a = 0, \dots, d-1 \right\}.$$

In this basis, the restricted Weil explicit form evaluates to:
$$\langle u_+^{(a)}, W u_+^{(b)} \rangle = 0, \quad \langle u_-^{(a)}, W u_-^{(b)} \rangle = 0,$$
$$\langle u_+^{(a)}, W u_-^{(b)} \rangle = (-1)^a \delta_{a, b}.$$

Thus, the matrix representation $W_d|_{\mathcal{V}_d} \in \mathbb{R}^{2d \times 2d}$ takes the block form:
$$W_d|_{\mathcal{V}_d} = \begin{pmatrix} \mathbf{0} & \mathbf{J}_d \\ \mathbf{J}_d & \mathbf{0} \end{pmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{J}_d = \operatorname{diag}\big(1, -1, 1, -1, \dots, (-1)^{d-1}\big).$$

### 4.3 Sylvester Inertia Spectrum
The characteristic polynomial factors into $d$ independent $2 \times 2$ anti-diagonal blocks:
$$\det\left(W_d - \lambda \mathbf{I}\right) = \prod_{a=0}^{d-1} \det \begin{pmatrix} -\lambda & (-1)^a \\ (-1)^a & -\lambda \end{pmatrix} = \prod_{a=0}^{d-1} (\lambda^2 - 1) = (\lambda - 1)^d (\lambda + 1)^d.$$

Consequently:
- Exactly $d$ eigenvalues equal $+1$.
- Exactly $d$ eigenvalues equal $-1$.
- Exactly $0$ eigenvalues equal $0$.

**Theorem 2.** For every jet height $d \ge 1$:
$$\operatorname{In}\left(W_d|_{\mathcal{V}_d}\right) = (n_+, n_-, n_0) = (d, d, 0).$$
As $d \to \infty$, the negative inertia defect $n_-(d) = d \to \infty$.
