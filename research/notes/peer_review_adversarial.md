## 2. Adversarial Stress-Test 1: 12-Gap Configuration for $M_{13}$

### 2.1 Problem Formulation & Mathematical Incommensurability
The $13 \times 13$ Gram matrix $M_{13}(g_1, \dots, g_{12})$ is generated by 12 consecutive positive gaps $y_i = \sum_{j=1}^i g_j$ ($y_0 = 0$) under the sinc-superposition kernel:
$$k(x) = \frac{\operatorname{sinc}\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}-2\pi x}{2}\right) + \operatorname{sinc}\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}+2\pi x}{2}\right)}{2 K_0}, \quad K_0 = \sqrt{2}\sin(1/\sqrt{2}) \approx 0.919395$$
The spectral defect functional $\Psi(\lambda)$ penalizes deviations from the identity matrix:
$$\Psi(\lambda) = \begin{cases} (\lambda - 1)^2 & \text{if } \lambda \le 2 \\ 2\lambda - 3 & \text{if } \lambda > 2 \end{cases}$$
To achieve $\operatorname{tr}\Psi(M_{13}) = 0$, all $\binom{13}{2} = 78$ pairwise off-diagonal entries $k(y_j - y_i)$ must simultaneously vanish. However, the roots of $k(x)$ in $(0, 13]$:
$$z_1 \approx 1.057278, \quad z_2 \approx 2.030068, \quad z_3 \approx 3.020275, \quad z_4 \approx 4.015243, \quad \dots$$
are strictly **sum-incommensurate**:
$$2 z_1 - z_2 = 2(1.057278) - 2.030068 = +0.084488 \neq 0$$
$$3 z_1 - z_3 = 3(1.057278) - 3.020275 = +0.151559 \neq 0$$

## 3. Adversarial Stress-Test 2: 6-Gap 2-Tower Augmented Matrix $M_{\text{aug}, 7}$

### 3.1 2-Tower Reproducing Kernel Mechanism
The 2-tower system couples the primary zero evaluation $\xi(s)$ with its first derivative $\xi'(s)$ on the critical line. The block-diagonal augmented matrix is:
$$M_{\text{aug}, 7} = \begin{pmatrix} M_{00} & 0 \\ 0 & M_{11} \end{pmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^{14 \times 14}$$
where:
- $M_{00}[i,j] = \frac{K_{00}(y_j - y_i)}{K_{00}(0)}$, with $K_{00}(x) = J_0(2\pi x - \sqrt{2}) + J_0(2\pi x + \sqrt{2})$ and $J_0(w) = \frac{\sin(w/2)}{w}$.
- $M_{11}[i,j] = \frac{K_{11}(y_j - y_i)}{K_{11}(0)}$, with $K_{11}(x) = J_2(2\pi x - \sqrt{2}) + J_2(2\pi x + \sqrt{2})$ and $J_2(w) = \frac{(w^2 - 8)\sin(w/2) + 4w\cos(w/2)}{4w^3}$.
- Normalizations: $K_{00}(0) \approx 0.91939539$, $K_{11}(0) \approx 0.07727142$.

### 3.2 Cross-Derivative Rigidity
Because $J_2(w)$ represents the Fourier transform of $t^2 \cos(\sqrt{2}t)$, its roots are intrinsically shifted relative to $J_0(w)$. Attempting to tune gap coordinates $y_j - y_i$ to cancel off-diagonal elements in $M_{00}$ places those exact distances near the antinodes of $M_{11}$, causing massive eigenvalue dispersion in the derivative block.
