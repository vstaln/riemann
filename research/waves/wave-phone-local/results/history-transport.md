### Episode 1 — Sphere packing: the LP (Delsarte/linear programming bound) as the new object (CONJECTURED history, high confidence in shape)

**(a) The ceiling.** After Rogers' 1958 upper bound, the ratio bound (the best proved upper density for high dimensions for decades) crept up only by sharper inequalities *inside* the same ball-packing/overlap-counting framework; in low dimensions, human/computer constructions (kissing-number and packing records via simulated annealing — e.g. Lindsay's 24D packing, Conway–Sloane's work) crept up slowly. Kepler (1611) in 3D resisted all direct attempts for ~390 years, and the "obvious" in-class move — improving the local density bound per ball — provably cannot reach the truth (the Voronoi/local analysis has a strict gap).
**(b) The new object.** The **Delsarte / linear-programming (LP) bound for sphere codes** (Delsarte 1972; applied to sphere packing by Levenshtein; made spectral by Cohn–Elkies 2003): a radial, Fourier-positive test function f with f(0) = 1, f ≤ 0 outside the unit ball, f̂ ≥ 0 yields an upper density bound via **positivity** — no geometric case analysis at all. The E8 (8D, 2009 Cohn–Kumar) and Leech (24D, 2016 Cohn–Kumar–Miller–Radchenko–Viazovska) exact solutions were *universal optimality* results: an **explicit auxiliary function with a modular-form / Eisenstein / linear-programming certificate** (Viazovska's magic functions built from modular forms in 8D/24D; the Cohn–Kumar 24D LP bound at the Leech density). The new object was the *certificate function* — a positivity witness — not a sharper density inequality.
**(c) In-hindsight diagnostic that the class was exhausted.** Every local-density/overlap improvement plateaued strictly below the conjectured optimum even though the *constructions* (E8, Leech) were known for decades — i.e., the upper-bound machinery and the extremal objects lived in different worlds; the extremal object (the lattice) was never the optimizer of the local bound. The wall looked structural (improvements decoupled from the known optima) and was.
**(d) Transport.** The analogue of the Delsarte certificate is our **Weil-form / LP-dual certificate** — and our program already *is* the Delsarte move (the LP-dual r ≈ 1−x is the positivity witness). The episode's real transport is: in sphere packing, the new objects that broke the next wall were **explicit auxiliary functions with extra structure** (modular forms in 8D/24D; the Poisson-summation/Eisenstein identity). For us: **build explicit certificate functions with a proven structural identity** — the sine-kernel reproducing kernel (Christoffel–Darboux form, determinantal structure) rather than the Gabor Gram W_T, or a modular/automorphic weight. ### Episode 2 — The prime number theorem: the zeta function / contour integration as the new object (CONJECTURED history, very high confidence in shape)

**(a) The ceiling.** PNT conjectured by Gauss/Legendre ~1790s; Chebyshev (1852) proved the right order and partial estimates (limits of the prime-counting ratio) using elementary/binomial arguments; the best elementary in-class tools (Chebyshev, Sylvester, elementary sieve bounds) could not separate the constant 1 — the class of "elementary counting arguments" was stuck.
**(b) The new object.** **Complex analysis applied to the Riemann zeta function**: Riemann (1859) introduced ζ as a complex function with an analytic continuation and functional equation; Hadamard and de la Vallée Poussin (1896) proved PNT by establishing the **zero-free region** Re(s) ≥ 1 − c/log t and using contour integration / Perron-type inversion of ζ′/ζ. The new object was the *complex function itself and the residue/contour calculus attached to it* — a transfer of the problem to a world with structure (analytic continuation, zeros, residues) the counting world lacked. Newman (1980) reduced the whole thing to one Tauberian step (the analytic continuation of ζ′/ζ past Re(s) = 1).
**(c) Diagnostic.** The class was exhausted because the *right object* was identified *before* the proof: Riemann's 1859 paper already contained the correct formulation, and the 37-year gap (1859→1896) was purely the missing zero-free region — every elementary improvement had hit the same wall, and the community knew the complex route was the only one left (the object was right, the data — a zero-free region — was missing).
**(d) Transport.** Our certificate already found its in-class right object (the LP-dual; the shadow-price-1 structure says the certificate is Newman-minimal: the object is right, the DATA is missing). ### Episode 4 — Transcendence: Baker's theorem / linear forms in logarithms as the new object (CONJECTURED history — LOW confidence in details, HIGH confidence in shape)
