=== B6.2: beyond-1 sampled rows vs periodicity of the 256-law ===

Identity: for a 256-periodic marked law with total mark mass 256,
S(j+256) = S(j) + 256 for all j  (periodicity + normalization),
so if rows 1..255 are near-CUE (256*S(j)/256 ~ j) then rows 257..512 are
automatically near-CUE as well.  The beyond-1 *sampled* rows add no new
constraint on the law; its simple fraction p1 is unchanged.
Check at j = 5:  S(5+256)/256 - (5+256)/256 = (S(5)+256)/256 - 261/256
               = S(5)/256 - 5/256  -> same deviation as at j=5.  (exact)

                       max |dev| over j=1..255 = 1.275e+02
                       at j=255 = -1.275e+02
