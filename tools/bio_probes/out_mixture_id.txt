=== B3.1 (a) count-level mixture model ===
mixture family from (tr,HS2)=(N,4N/3) with c=1: a2 in [0,1.00], p = (N/3 - 2*a2)/2 >= 0
  m3/N ranges over [1.0000, 2.0000] across the family (all-simple a2=0 -> m3/N=1 ; extremal a2=N/6 -> m3/N=2)
  -> two moments leave a 1-parameter family; the third moment (skewness)
     completes identification: this is the mixture-identifiability structure.
  -> but note: in the IDEAL model m3 is blind to p (planes cancel); the
     off-diagonal cross-terms are exactly what the real data add (part b).
